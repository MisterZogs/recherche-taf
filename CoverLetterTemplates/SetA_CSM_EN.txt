The other half is testing early. Rather than saving validation for one large go-live at the end, we tested each piece as it was configured, so corrections stayed small and the customer saw progress along the way.

The hardest part for customers is the work the project asks of them: cleaning their data, answering the questions only they can answer, and taking decisions. What worked was naming a key user per country early and training them while the system was still being configured rather than at the end, then keeping a written record of every decision so none of it was reopened three months later.
